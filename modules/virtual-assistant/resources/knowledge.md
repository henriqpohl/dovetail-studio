HOW WE WORK: THE DOUBLE DIAMOND
Four phases. Discover: market research, user research, managing information, design research groups; we audit the existing brand and research the sector, competitors and audience. Define: project development, project management and project sign-off; the strategic direction is agreed with the client in writing before any design starts. Develop: multi-disciplinary working, visual management, development methods and testing; considered design routes, each tested at small sizes, in single colour, reversed and on real applications. Deliver: final testing, approval and launch, then targets, evaluation and feedback loops; final artwork in every format, brand guidelines and a rollout plan.
Every logo is tested at small sizes, in mono, reversed and in print before sign-off. One team handles print and digital, so the brand stays consistent across both. Artwork files are kept on record for future reprints and changes. Rollout of a new identity is usually phased so old and new are not seen side by side.

LOGO AND BRAND IDENTITY: WHAT IS INCLUDED
Brand audit and competitor research; strategic direction agreed in writing; logo and identity design with a rationale for each route; full colour palette in Pantone, CMYK, RGB and HEX; typography system with licensing checked; vector masters (AI, EPS, SVG) plus print-ready files; full-colour, single-colour and reversed versions; brand guidelines covering clear space, minimum sizes and applications; rollout sequencing across touchpoints.
Rebrand versus refresh: a rebrand replaces the identity, a refresh evolves it. Rebrand when the business has changed; refresh when the business is the same but the identity has aged. Handled properly (carrying forward recognisable elements, announcing the change), a rebrand does not lose customers.

PRINT AND BROCHURES: DETAIL
End to end: concept, copy layout, finished artwork, print management and delivery. Paper weight, fold and ink decisions are made at artwork stage. Files go to press with correct trim, 3mm bleed, CMYK or Pantone, 300dpi images, embedded fonts, checked page by page. We can pre-flight artwork supplied by others.
Common formats: A4 (210 x 297mm), A5 (148 x 210mm), DL (99 x 210mm). Folds: bi-fold (4 panels), tri-fold or roll-fold (6 panels), Z-fold, gate-fold. Larger documents saddle-stitched or perfect-bound.

WEBSITES: DETAIL
Brand-led WordPress websites planned and built by the same team that does the brand, not templates. Planning, design, build, launch; WooCommerce for shops; SEO and analytics set-up; hosting, maintenance and content support afterwards. Existing sites can be redesigned or maintained.
